# Validation Interview Guide (recommended next step — not conducted)

Purpose: test the Phase 2 hypotheses with primary evidence. **No interviews have been conducted for this case study, and no interview findings appear anywhere in it.**

## Sample design (suggested)
| Group | n | Who | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Industrial distributors | 8–10 | VP Sales, sales ops or marketing leads at $20M–$500M electrical, PHVAC, MRO and machinery distributors | H3, H4, H6, willingness to pay |
| Distribution-focused agencies / consultants | 3–4 | Firms implementing CRM or GTM tooling for distributors | Delivery model, partner economics |
| Professional-services firms | 5–6 | Marketing / BD leaders at A&E, IT services and consulting firms | H9, incremental headroom, substitutes |
| Clay power users in industrials | 2–3 | Teams like the Regency Supply case | Time-to-value, required templates |

## Core questions — distributors
1. How do your reps decide which new accounts to pursue each month? Who builds the list?
2. Where do your best prospect lists come from today (CRM, ERP, ZoomInfo/Apollo, Google, trade associations, permits, Dodge)? What share of target contractors can you reach with a direct phone or email?
3. Walk me through the last time a rep logged a new contact in the CRM. What happened?
4. Who internally could build or maintain an automated prospecting workflow? If no one, who would you call?
5. What do you spend today per year on prospect data and sales tools? Who approves it?
6. If every outside rep could get a scored, enriched list of contractors in their territory each week, what would that be worth? What would make you not use it?
7. Which systems must anything new connect to (ERP name and version, CRM)?

## Core questions — professional services
1. Who does business development, and how much of their time goes to research versus selling?
2. Which tools do you use for prospect data and outreach (Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clay, internal builds)? Why those?
3. Have you used Clay? If so, for what, and how did the 2026 pricing change affect you?
4. What would make you add, expand or drop a GTM data/workflow tool in the next 12 months?

## Coverage test (quantitative companion)
Draw a random sample of ~200 contractors from public licensing boards in 2–3 states. Measure verified email and phone match rates across Apollo, ZoomInfo and a Clay waterfall (including Google Maps). This directly measures H3, which Phase 2 only estimated with a proxy.
